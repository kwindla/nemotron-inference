Reconciliation notes
--------------------

- `NanoP1TiledMma` uses the cooperative SM120 block-scaled geometry: `AtomLayoutMNK = (_4,_2,_1)` and `ValLayoutMNK = Tile<128, ((_8,_2,_2), stride (1,16,8)), 64>`, which yields a `128x128x128` CTA tile over `256` threads with `32` threads per atom.
- `NanoP1SmemLayoutA/B` use the canonical 4-bit UMMA shared-memory swizzle that actually divides `K=128` in this CUTLASS snapshot: `UMMA::Layout_K_SW64_Atom<uint4_t>`, staged with `tile_to_shape(..., Step<_1,_2,_3>)` for A and `Step<_2,_1,_3>` for B. That produces the expected total cosize `65536` for four `128x128` stages.
- `NanoP1SmemLayoutSFA/SFB` come from `cutlass::detail::Sm1xxBlockScaledConfig<16>::tile_atom_to_shape_SFA/SFB` on the `128x128x128` CTA tile with `L=4` stages, which yields the expected `4096`-element scale shared-memory surfaces.
- `tCrA_k0` / `tCrB_k0` were measured from the stage-0 shared-memory slices with the same operand convention the reverted reference used for this P1 geometry: `partition_fragment_B(sA_stage0)` gives the activation-thread fragment `(16,8)`, and `partition_fragment_A(sB_stage0)` gives the weight-thread fragment `(32,2)`.
- `tCrSFA` / `tCrSFB` were measured with temporary builder-free helpers that mirror the CUTLASS `sm120_blockscaled_mma_tma.hpp` scale-fragment partition logic without instantiating any forbidden runtime `Collective*` surface. Those helpers produced `(64,2,2)` and `(64,8,2)`, matching the reverted reference exactly.
- `tCrC_profile = (4,2,8)` comes from `cute::partition_fragment_C(NanoP1TiledMma{}, make_shape(_128{}, _128{}))` and matches the TRT-LLM P1 accumulator-profile target.
